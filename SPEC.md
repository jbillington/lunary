# LUNARY
### The Game of Growing Ideas Under the Moon

**One file containing:** Game Description · Rulebook · User Manual with Example of Play · Product Requirements Document
**Version 1.0 · September 2026**

> *"Every idea is a seed. Tend what the moon favors.*
> *Let each project become more itself."*

**On the name.** A *lunary* (Latin *lunarium*, also "moonbook") is a real medieval genre:
a book of prognostication organized by the thirty days of the moon's cycle, telling the
reader what to undertake on each — when to travel, when to trade, when to plant. The
game is that book, kept for projects instead of crops.

*Lunaria* is also the moon-plant, whose translucent silver seedpods growers call
**moonpennies** — the game's one currency (§8.9). Its perennial species is
*Lunaria rediviva*: "brought back to life," which is what the Conservatory is for.

# PART I — THE GAME DESCRIPTION

## 1. What This Is

Lunary is a solo ritual card game for software makers who want to ship on a
monthly rhythm. Each project is a living card. Each new moon, you perform a divination —
drawing a Planet and a Spirit — that tells you what energy the project needs and what
work will move it forward. Each full moon, you listen to what the project whispered back
(evidence from the real world) and let it grow one step along its phase track.

It is not a productivity system. It is closer to tarot for your projects: the cards don't
decide for you — they clarify, focus, provoke, and generate the next idea you wouldn't
have had on your own. The discipline comes from the moon's rhythm; the creativity comes
from the draw.

## 2. The Philosophy

1. **Every idea is good.** No idea is ever killed, rejected, or thrown away. An idea
   either has the spotlight, waits warmly, rests, or becomes an elder that teaches.
2. **Iteration, not judgment.** A project's job is to become more itself — more aligned
   with its users, more valuable — one moon at a time.
3. **Evidence is listening, not scoring.** Real-world signals (a stranger used it,
   someone paid, someone wrote back) are "moonpennies." Moonpennies do not pass or fail a project;
   they tell you which way it wants to grow.
4. **The deck divines; the cards keep diaries.** You are the gardener and the reader,
   not the judge. Over months, each project card's back fills with the chronicle of its
   own life, written by the spirits that visited it.
5. **Scarcity comes from your hands, not from rejection.** The garden has unlimited
   room for sleeping projects, but only **three beds** can be actively tended — because
   you have two hands and one heart.

## 3. The Setting: The Garden

The play mat (or tablecloth, or cleared desk) is laid out as a garden under an
observatory sky. It has five zones:

| Zone | What lives there | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **The Seed Vault** | Ideas written on slips, not yet cards | Every idea waits warmly; none is rejected |
| 🌿 **The Garden Beds** (max 3) | Projects in active iteration | Tended, visited monthly by spirits |
| 🏡 **The Conservatory** | Resting projects | Sleeping through winter |
| 🌳 **The Grove** | Projects whose purpose is complete | Elders. They taught you something and now give counsel |
| 🌕 **The Harvest Shelf** | Shipped & alive in the world | Your growing trophy shelf |

## 4. The Decks

| Sub-deck | Count | Back color | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Project cards** | 1 per project (grows over years) | Cream | The living beings |
| **Planet deck** | 10 | Indigo | The *energy* infusing a project this moon |
| **Spirit deck** | 16 | Violet | The *visitor* — brings a gift, an invitation, a twist |
| **Itch deck** | 12 | Dusk-pink | Drawn only when a project feels stuck |

Plus: **seed slips** (cheap paper), the **Principle card** (the rules of the garden,
always visible), a **phase marker** per project, **moonpennies** (silver tokens), a
**date stamp**, **grease pencil**, **wax seal**, and **one candle**.

## 5. The Rhythm of Play

- **Every month, two ceremonies:**
  - 🌑 **New Moon — "Invocation"** (~20 min): set the garden, divine for each tended
    project, schedule the work, seal the vow.
  - 🌕 **Full Moon — "Communion"** (~20 min): bring evidence, listen, advance phases.
- **Every quarter (solstice/equinox):** the **Deep Reading** — a five-card portfolio
  spread across the whole garden.
- **Every year (winter solstice):** the **Long Night** — consult the Grove, write the
  year's lessons as new hand-made Spirit cards, and shuffle them permanently into the
  deck. **The deck physically grows with you every year.**

# PART II — THE RULES

## 1. Components

- 1 play mat printed as the garden map (zones + Deep Reading spread outline)
- Project cards (tarot size, $70 \times 120$ mm, matte — must accept pencil)
- Planet deck (10), Spirit deck (16), Itch deck (12)
- 1 Principle card, 1 phase marker per project, moonpenny tokens
- Date stamp, grease pencil, wax seal kit, seed slips, one candle

## 2. Project Card Anatomy

**Front**
- Name
- One-line purpose
- Target user

**Back**
- **Current test** — the single riskiest assumption being tested this cycle
- **Success signal** — observable evidence + a number ("3 of 10 testers open it twice")
- **Phase track** — five moon icons: 🌑 Seed → 🌒 Waxing → 🌕 Full → 🌘 Waning → 🏡 Resting,
  with a movable marker
- **Chronicle band** — a writable strip; one line per cycle, never erased
- **Moonpenny log** — silver tokens for real-world signals, with dates
- **Last visited** — which spirit/planet came last (enforces the no-repeat rule)

## 3. The Phase Track & Movement Legality

```
🌑 Seed ──→ 🌒 Waxing ──→ 🌕 Full ──→ 🌘 Waning ──→ 🏡 Resting
                  ↑                                  │
                  └──────────── (wake) ──────────────┘
```

- Phases move **forward or sideways only — never backward.** Iteration is always
  progress; a rest is a season like any other.
- **Seed → Waxing:** the idea is adopted into a Garden bed and work begins.
- **Waxing:** building toward first contact with real users.
- **Full:** it exists in the world and is being used by someone who isn't you.
- **Waning:** harvesting — revenue, feedback, templates, lessons. (Optional phase.)
- **Resting:** asleep in the Conservatory. Any Resting project may be **woken at any
  new moon** — no permission needed, no penalty.
- **Grove & Harvest Shelf** are **zone transfers**, not phases: a project is moved
  there by your decision at any Communion, and its card keeps its full chronicle.

## 4. The New Moon Ceremony — "Invocation"

Performed at (or near) the new moon. Candle lit. ~20 minutes. In order:

1. **Prepare.** Lay the mat. Light the candle in the same spot every time.
2. **Read the Principle aloud.**
3. **Review the garden.** Look at every zone. Recall last cycle's moonpennies.
4. **Move the garden.** For each project, decide: advance a phase, hold, enter Resting,
   wake a sleeper, move a completed project to the Grove, move a shipped project to the
   Harvest Shelf.
5. **Welcome new ideas.** Write new ideas on **seed slips** (name + one line). A slip
   may be **adopted** into a Garden bed — this **costs a bed slot** (see Hands Limit,
   §8.1) and moves the project to 🌑 Seed phase. To adopt, the slip must win its pitch
   (§8.4) if all beds are full.
6. **Divine for each tended project.** For every project in a Garden bed:
   1. Hold the project card and ask aloud: *"What do you need to become more yourself?"*
   2. Draw **1 Planet + 1 Spirit** (filtered to exclude last cycle's Spirit for that
      project — the No-Repeat rule).
   3. If the draw feels wrong, you may **redraw once.** If you redraw a second time,
      **the third draw must be kept.**
   4. Read the Planet's energy, then the Spirit's Gift, Invitation, and Twist.
   5. **Read the state line:** find the line on the Spirit card matching the project's
      current phase (Waxing / Full / Resting). The same card means something different
      given the state — this is the divination.
   6. **Schedule:** every Spirit names its planetary day (e.g., Mercury → Wednesday).
      Write the Invitation into your real calendar on **that day** of the coming weeks.
   7. **Write the chronicle line** on the card's band: *"Cycle 7 — visited by Mercury:
      we will say it in one sentence."*
   8. **Seal:** sign or wax-seal the card. That is the vow.
7. **Return the Spirit and Planet cards** to their decks. A new one arrives next moon.

## 5. Between Moons — The Planetary Days

The work happens on the days the visiting spirits named:

| Day | Planet | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | ☉ Sun | Radiance — being seen |
| Monday | ☽ Moon | Listening — users, hunches |
| Tuesday | ♂ Mars | Ignition — the scary small ship |
| Wednesday | ☿ Mercury | Words — clarity, exchange |
| Thursday | ♃ Jupiter | Expansion — give more than asked |
| Friday | ♀ Venus | Beauty — desire, delight |
| Saturday | ♄ Saturn | Structure — roots, rhythm, infrastructure |

The three modern planets (♅ Uranus, ♆ Neptune, ♇ Pluto) have no fixed day; perform
their work on any day of the cycle.

## 6. The Full Moon Ceremony — "Communion"

At (or near) the full moon. Bring the receipts: numbers, screenshots, quotes.

1. For each tended project, hold the card up and state its evidence aloud like
   testimony: *"What did you whisper back this moon?"*
2. **Strike moonpennies:** silver tokens for real-world signals — a stranger used it,
   someone paid, unsolicited feedback, a returning user. Date each moonpenny.
3. **Move the phase marker** one step forward, or hold, or enter Resting. Never backward.
4. **Thank the visiting spirit** and return its card. (No project receives the same
   spirit twice in a row — the deck keeps rotating perspectives at you.)
5. Photograph the layout if you keep a digital record.

## 7. Seasonal & Annual Ceremonies

**🌗 The Deep Reading** (each solstice/equinox, ~45 min). Shuffle the Spirit deck and
lay five cards across the garden:

1. **The Root** — what grounds this season
2. **The Weather** — what is coming at you
3. **The Current** — what is growing fastest
4. **The Crossroads** — the choice this season offers
5. **The Seed Underground** — what wants to be started

Then assign seasons: who wakes, who rests, who joins the Grove.

**🎄 The Long Night** (winter solstice, ~1 hr). Consult the Grove: each Elder answers
one question ("what should I carry forward from you?"). Write the year's lessons onto
**new hand-made Spirit cards** and shuffle them permanently into the deck. An Elder may
be **awakened**: a new seed may inherit an Elder's power as a remix, a lineage — the
Elder's essence returns in a new card.

## 8. Special Rules

### 8.1 The Hands Limit
You may tend at most **3 Garden beds** per cycle. Not because ideas compete — because
*you* have two hands. Everything else waits warmly in the Vault or sleeps in the
Conservatory.

### 8.2 The No-Repeat Rule
A project cannot receive the same Spirit two cycles running. Filter the deck before the
draw. (Planets may repeat.)

### 8.3 The Rerule
One voluntary redraw is always allowed. If you redraw a second time, the third draw must
be kept. (Classic divination: agency, but no infinite reshuffling until you like it.)

### 8.4 Adopting a Seed Slip (the Pitch)
If a Garden bed is free, any slip may be adopted freely. If all three beds are full,
the slip must win a **pitch-off** against the bed you'd displace: one page — the idea,
one metric, why it wins the bed *this season*. The loser returns to the Vault. No
hard feelings: the Vault is warm.

### 8.5 Feeling Stuck (the Itch)
Once per cycle, any tended project may draw **one Itch card** — a pure provocation.
Perform it or let it spark a better idea; either way, note it in the chronicle.

### 8.6 Resting & Waking
A project that stays quiet for two cycles (no moonpennies, no invitation completed) is
**invited** into the Conservatory — never forced. Resting projects draw 🌙 Moon or ♄
Saturn if divined (their waxing/full lines don't apply). Any Resting project may be
woken at any new moon.

### 8.7 The Grove & Harvest
- **Harvest Shelf:** the project ships publicly and is alive in the world. A gilt moonpenny on
  the card. It may still be woken for a sequel or pivot.
- **Grove:** the project's *purpose is complete* (learned what it came to teach, even if
  it never shipped big). Its card becomes an **Elder**: consultable yearly, awakenable
  forever. Elders compost into the next generation — ideas never die here.

### 8.8 Custom Spirits
At the Long Night, hand-written Spirit cards enter the permanent deck, tagged
`custom: true`. They follow all spirit rules (including no-repeat).

### 8.9 Moonpennies — the only currency
*Lunaria's* seedpods are translucent silver discs; growers call them **moonpennies**.
Each is a coin and a seed packet at once, and that double nature is the rule.

**Striking.** You strike one moonpenny onto a project each time the world answers it —
a stranger used it, someone paid, someone wrote back unprompted, a user returned. Only
the world mints them. You cannot award a moonpenny for effort, for shipping, or for a
good intention; if nobody outside you moved, no penny is struck.

**Reading.** Moonpennies do not score a project. They are a direction-finder: where the
pennies land is where the thing wants to grow. A project with none is not failing — it
is unanswered, which is information of a different kind.

**Sowing (the second nature).** A moonpenny is a seedpod, so it can be spent as one. At
the Long Night, any project may **sow** its moonpennies: each one spent becomes a seed
slip in the Vault, carrying a line of what that answer taught. Evidence funds the next
generation of ideas. A project that the world answered loudly leaves more behind it than
one that was merely finished.

Sown pennies leave the project's log — the chronicle records what they became. This is
the only way a project's record ever gets shorter, and it is a gift, not a loss.

## 9. What Winning Means

There is no score and no end state. The garden is healthy when:
- A **real product step ships each moon** — announced, in the world, however small.
- Every tended project has a **dated, scheduled invitation** each cycle — and most of
  them get done.
- The **chronicles grow** — cards visibly age, and rereading a card's back tells the
  story of its life.
- The **Harvest Shelf and Grove keep filling** — year over year.

# PART III — USER MANUAL & EXAMPLE OF PLAY

*This walkthrough shows three full cycles with a real project. Follow along as if you
were playing. Times shown are the recommended ceremony length.*

## Before You Begin — First Setup (~45 min, once)

1. Lay the mat on your table. Zones left → right: Seed Vault, Garden Beds, Conservatory,
   Grove, Harvest Shelf. Put the Principle card at the top center.
2. Shuffle Planet, Spirit, and Itch decks separately. Place them at the corners of the mat.
3. Write your first projects on **seed slips** — name and one line each. For this
   example, we have three:
   - *Ask Dad — Mac cleaner & troubleshooting*
   - *Moonmark — a habit tracker for night owls*
   - *Postcard — a static-site newsletter tool*
4. Adopt up to three slips into Garden beds. Move each project card (hand-written for
   now — ship v1 ugly) to 🌑 Seed. Fill in front fields: name, purpose, target user.
5. Stamp the date on each card. You're ready.

## CYCLE 1 — New Moon, Friday, September 11, 2026 (Invocation, ~20 min)

> **Step 1.** Light the candle. Same spot, same candle, every cycle.
>
> **Step 2.** Read the Principle card aloud: *"Every idea is a seed. Tend what the
> moon favors. Let each project become more itself."*
>
> **Step 3.** Review the garden. All three projects sit at 🌑 Seed — first cycle, no
> moonpennies yet. All three beds are tended (3 = the Hands Limit, exactly full).
>
> **Step 4. Divine for Ask Dad.** Hold the card. Ask aloud:
> *"Ask Dad, what do you need to become more yourself?"*
>
> **Draw** (left hand): Planet **☿ Mercury** — words, clarity, exchange.
> Spirit: **The Thief** — *steal a mechanic from a product you admire.* Hmm. It feels
> wrong for a first cycle — there's nothing to steal from yet. **Rerule once:**
>
> **Draw 2:** Planet **☿ Mercury** (again — planets may repeat), Spirit
> **The Cartographer** — *map the user's journey; mark where they stray.* This one
> sings. Keep it.
>
> **Step 5. Read the state line.** Ask Dad is 🌑 Seed. The Cartographer's seed-phase
> line reads: *"Draw the journey as your dad would walk it: from 'my Mac is slow' to
> 'it's fast.' Mark every place he'd stall."*
>
> **Step 6. Schedule.** The Cartographer's planetary day is **Wednesday**. Open your
> calendar and block **Wednesday, September 16** — *"Ask Dad: map Dad's journey, mark
> 3 stalls."*
>
> **Step 7. Write the chronicle line** on the card's band, in pencil:
> `C1 · Sep 11 — Mercury + The Cartographer: map Dad's journey; mark where he stalls.`
>
> **Step 8.** Wax-seal the corner. The vow is made.
>
> **Step 9. Divine for Moonmark and Postcard** the same way. Example results:
> - *Moonmark:* ☉ Sun + **The Bell-Ringer** → Sunday: design the moment the app
>   announces itself. State line (Seed): *"Make the welcome screen a single bell."*
> - *Postcard:* ♄ Saturn + **The Flame-Keeper** → Saturday: nothing new enters; set the
>   weekly publishing rhythm. State line (Seed): *"One page published weekly beats ten
>   planned."*

**Ceremony ends.** Photograph the layout if you keep records. Blow out the candle.

## CYCLE 1 — The Work Between Moons (planetary days)

- **Sun, Sep 13** — Moonmark's work: sketch the bell welcome screen (30 min).
- **Wed, Sep 16** — Ask Dad's work: hand-draw Dad's journey map; three stalls marked
  ("finds the app", "trusts it enough to run it", "knows it worked").
- **Sat, Sep 19** — Postcard's work: publish page one; set the weekly Saturday slot.
- **Tue, Sep 22** — (no invitation required Mars work; but you ship Ask Dad's first
  clickable prototype anyway — small and embarrassing, per tradition.)

## CYCLE 1 — Full Moon, Saturday, September 26 (Communion, ~20 min)

> **Step 1.** Light the candle. Bring receipts: the journey map photo, the prototype
> link, one text from your brother ("dad would actually use this").
>
> **Step 2.** For each project, hold it up: *"What did you whisper back this moon?"*
> - **Ask Dad:** the prototype ran once on a real dad's Mac. → *Place one moonpenny:*
>   ◎ *Sep 23 — first real-dad run.*
> - **Moonmark:** sketch exists, nobody else saw it. → no moonpennies. Hold.
> - **Postcard:** page one is live; two friends read it. → ◎ *Sep 20 — first readers.*
>
> **Step 3. Move phases.** Ask Dad: 🌑 Seed → 🌒 Waxing (it exists; contact made).
> Postcard: 🌑 Seed → 🌒 Waxing. Moonmark: hold at Seed — it's early, and early is fine.
>
> **Step 4.** Thank each spirit aloud; return Planet and Spirit cards to their decks.
> Stamp today's date on every card. **The cards now visibly age.**

## CYCLE 2 — New Moon, October 10 (the draw that changes direction)

The garden reviews. Ask Dad and Postcard are 🌒 Waxing. Moonmark, quiet two cycles, is
**invited** into the Conservatory — you tuck it in with the Moon card laid on top.
"Wake when you're ready."

**Divination for Ask Dad:** draw Planet **♀ Venus** — beauty, desire, attraction.
Spirit: **The Lighthouse** — *remove everything that dims the one promise.*
State line (Waxing): *"Strip the app to the one light: cleaning. Hide the rest."*

The twist generates the idea you wouldn't have had: instead of building more features,
you spend the cycle **removing** two of the three screens. The chronicle line:

`C2 · Oct 10 — Venus + The Lighthouse: strip to one light. Done Oct 17: settings gone.`

You also write a new seed slip during the month: *Grandpa Mode — voice-guided Mac help
for the least tech-forward.* It goes to the **Vault**. All beds are full; it waits
warmly. Maybe next season it pitches for a bed.

## CYCLE 2 — Full Moon, October 25

- **Ask Dad:** the stripped build ran on two more dads' machines; one son wrote an
  unsolicited note: *"this is the first cleaner my dad let install."* Two moonpennies.
  Phase: 🌒 → 🌕 **Full.** It exists in the world for people who aren't you.
- **Postcard:** steady weekly publishing; three subscribers. One moonpenny. Hold at
  Waxing — Full means *strangers*, not friends. (Not a failure — a measurement.)

## CYCLE 3 — New Moon, November 9 (the draw that sends you sideways)

**Divination for Ask Dad** (Full phase now). Draw: Planet **♃ Jupiter** — expansion,
generosity. Spirit: **The Seedkeeper** — *condense what you learned into a template
others can use.* State line (Full): *"Give the journey map away. The map is the
product's twin."*

You schedule Thursday (Jupiter's day): package the journey map as a one-page PDF. It
becomes the most-shared thing you've made all year — and it feeds the project: a
newsletter reader asks if the *app* does the voice-guided thing. You smile and look at
the slip in the Vault: **Grandpa Mode is stirring.**

**Itch moment:** Postcard feels stuck mid-cycle. Once per cycle, you may draw the Itch
deck: **"Do it by hand for one user."** So you hand-write one subscriber's newsletter
layout for a month. It teaches you more than the last three features combined. Chronicle:
`C3 · Itch drawn — hand-laid Edna's issue. Lesson: layout is the product.`

## The Pattern, Summarized

| Cycle | New Moon (Invocation) | Work | Full Moon (Communion) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Draws → schedule Wednesday → seal | Journey map, first prototype | First moonpennies; 2 projects → Waxing |
| 2 | Venus + Lighthouse: remove | Strip to one light | → Full; strangers arrive |
| 3 | Jupiter + Seedkeeper: give the map away | PDF template; itch-draw | Map spreads; next generation stirs |

Three moons, three different energies, three different directions — each one arriving
from a draw you didn't plan, each one written permanently into the card's diary. That
is the game.

# PART IV — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT
### Lunary — Web-Based Playtest Simulation

**Version 0.1 · Date: 2026-09-11 · Status: Draft**

## 1. Summary

A single-page, offline-capable web app that faithfully simulates the Lunary card
game so the *game design itself* can be playtested before printing physical cards. All
state is local-first (browser storage + JSON export). Artwork is placeholder SVG
sketches behind a stable asset layer, so art can be upgraded continuously without
touching game logic.

**The real product being tested is not the app — it's the ritual.** The app must
therefore capture playtest evidence: were invitations scheduled? done? did cycles
produce shipped steps? did readings stay fresh?

## 2. Goals & Non-Goals

| Goals | Non-Goals |
|---|---|
| Enforce all game rules mechanically (phase tracks, hands limit, no-repeat, rerule) | No multiplayer, no accounts, no server |
| Guide both rituals step-by-step like a real ceremony | No AI generation of card content (content is authored, fixed) |
| Preserve the chronicle — every card's diary, exportable forever | No mobile-native app (responsive browser is enough) |
| 100% open-source stack, runs on Mac via `npm run dev` | No print-perfect PDFs in v1 (browser print is acceptable) |
| Sketch-grade art, swappable without code changes | No real astrology engine — one simple moon-phase algorithm |

## 3. Stack Decision

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Language | **TypeScript** (strict) | Rule enforcement wants types: `Planet`, `Spirit`, `Phase`, `Zone` |
| UI | **React 18 + Vite** | Fastest to iterate card components; huge ecosystem |
| State | **Zustand** | Tiny, testable, persists trivially |
| Persistence | **idb-keyval** (IndexedDB) + JSON export/import | Local-first; survives restarts; no backend |
| Art | **Inline SVG components** driven by content JSON | Sketch placeholders now; enhanced art drops in later |
| Moon phases | ~15-line synodic algorithm (no dependency) | Real phase display; rituals triggered manually |
| License | MIT, single repo | Everything above is MIT/BSD |

No backend. `npm run dev` on a Mac, open `localhost:5173`, play. Deployable later as
static files.

## 4. Rules the Simulation Must Enforce

1. Zones: Seed Vault (slips) · Garden beds (max **3** tended) · Conservatory · Grove ·
   Harvest Shelf.
2. Phase track moves: **forward or sideways only — never backward.** Grove/Harvest are
   zone transfers, not phases.
3. Invocation draw: per tended project, 1 Planet + 1 Spirit. One voluntary redraw; the
   **third draw must be kept**.
4. No-repeat: a project cannot receive the same Spirit two cycles running (deck is
   filtered before draw).
5. Planetary scheduling: each reading must produce a **dated calendar entry** on the
   spirit's planetary day.
6. Chronicle: every reading appends one immutable line to the project's chronicle band.
7. Moonpennies: token events attach to projects at Full Moon. Resting after two quiet
   cycles is invited, never forced.
8. Seed slips: lightweight (name + one line). Adoption costs a bed slot; pitch-off when
   beds are full.
9. Itch deck: drawable only via "Feeling stuck?", max once per project per cycle.
10. Custom spirits: the annual Long Night can add user-authored Spirit cards
    (`custom: true`).

## 6. Screens

1. **The Mat** (home) — five zones as a garden map; cards show name, phase icon, last
   spirit, moonpennies, chronicle count. The emotional centerpiece; must feel like a
   place, not a dashboard.
2. **Project Detail** — full card: purpose, target user, current test/signal, phase
   track, moonpenny log, chronicle timeline, "Feeling stuck?" button.
3. **Ritual Stepper** — full-screen ceremony mode; one step per screen, candle motif,
   large card reveals with flip animation, explicit "Schedule" and "Seal" actions.
4. **Communion Stepper** — moonpenny entry, phase moves, whispered-back text.

## 8. Art Pipeline

- Every card renders from content JSON through one `<GameCard>` SVG component; art
  lives in `src/art/` as named SVGs keyed by stable IDs (`planet-mercury.svg`).
- v1 art style: single-weight line sketches — moon phases, planetary sigils
  (☿ ♀ ♂ ♃ ♄ ☉ ☽ ♅ ♆ ♇), simple spirit glyphs. Deliberately rough; typography and
  layout carry the beauty first.
- Rule: **no art asset is referenced by path from logic** — upgrading art means
  replacing SVG files, zero code changes.
